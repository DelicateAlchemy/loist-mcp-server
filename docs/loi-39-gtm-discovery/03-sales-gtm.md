## 1. Market context (why now)

- Sync is the fastest-growing, most workflow-broken revenue line in publishing: ~17% of publishing revenue now comes from sync, and the sync segment is projected to grow ~28% annually through 2028 ([Dataintelo sync market](https://dataintelo.com/report/sync-licensing-market), [Medium/Davison 2025 sync review](https://medium.com/@htlympremium/how-sync-licensing-changed-in-2025-and-where-its-headed-in-2026-8db5149e666f)). Streamers invested $35B+ in original content in 2025, feeding sync fees to publishers and labels.
- The incumbent stack is a royalty/asset stack, not a *work management* stack: Vistex Counterpoint (30-year ERP incumbent) handles rights & royalties ([Vistex Counterpoint](https://www.vistex.com/product-suites/counterpoint-suite/)); Synchtank/DISCO/Source Audio handle catalog showcase and pitching ([Synchtank publishers](https://www.synchtank.com/solutions/music-publishers), [3 Theory on DISCO](https://3theorymusic.com/our-favorite-sync-licensing-tool-for-2021/)). **Nobody owns the clearance/task lifecycle (requested → negotiating → cleared/rejected/expired), split confirmation, or registration workflow — that lives in Outlook + Excel.** Synchtank's own blog sells "streamline your sync licensing workflows" content because the pain is universal ([Synchtank blog](https://www.synchtank.com/synchblog/5-ways-to-streamline-sync-licensing-workflows)).
- **M&A churn is reshaping the top 20% right now** — this is both the opening and the risk:
  - Primary Wave agreed to acquire Kobalt (announced 2026-03-23, closing ~Q3 2026, ~$7B combined) ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/primary-wave-to-acquire-kobalt-7-billion-company-1236696584/), [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/primary-wave-buys-kobalt/)).
  - Virgin Music Group (UMG) completed the $775M Downtown acquisition in Feb 2026; EC forced divestment of Curve Royalty Systems ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2026/02/20/virgin-music-group-completes-downtown-music-holdings-acquisition/), [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/virgin-music-group-completes-downtown-acquisition-pieter-van-rijn-named-coo-as-founder-justin-kalifowitz-steps-away/)).
  - Hipgnosis rebranded to Recognition Music Group (Mar 2025); Sony Music Publishing agreed to acquire the 45,000-song Recognition catalog ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/sony-music-publishing-confirms-agreement-to-acquire-blackstones-recognition-music-group-encompassing-over-45000-songs/), [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2025/03/12/three-hipgnosis-companies-rebrand-as-recognition-music-group/)).
  - Post-merger integrations = thousands of works changing hands = split re-confirmation, re-registration, and clearance chaos. **"Catalog migration workflow" is a killer wedge use case.**
- Publisher market share concentration (Q2 2025, Hot 100): Sony MP ~34%, Warner Chappell ~22%, UMPG ~19%, then Kobalt and BMG ([Billboard rankings](https://www.billboard.com/pro/music-publishers-quarterly-rankings-q2-2025-sony-charts/)). The "biggest 20%" therefore = 3 majors + ~25–35 large/mid independents and rights-management companies.

## 4. Campaign design (biggest-20% push)

### Phase sequencing (product-constrained: single-tenant today)
1. **Months 0–6 — Design partners (3–5, Tier A).** Single-tenant is fine — deploy per-customer instances. Charge (see §6): free products get ignored; paid design partners give feedback and become references. Target: Position Music, Third Side Music, Domino Publishing, one Beggars/Ninja Tune-type label group, one admin shop (Wixen/Exploration). Win theme: *clearance pipeline out of the inbox in 30 days*.
2. **Months 6–12 — Lighthouse logos (Tier B).** With multi-tenant + basic SOC 2 in flight, convert 2–3 design partners to case studies; open Reservoir, Concord, Round Hill, Anthem, Sentric, Spirit. Anchor proof: cycle-time reduction (quote→cleared), % quotes expired unactioned, split-confirmation turnaround.
3. **Months 12–18 — Category campaign.** Public launch around a sync-workflow benchmark report ("State of Sync Ops"); AIMP/IMPF panel circuit; press in MBW/Music Week/Synchtank blog. Begin major-publisher nurture (they will have watched the indies).
4. **Majors (18+ months).** Enter via one region/team (e.g., a European sync office of one major) rather than global deals.

### Channels that actually work in this industry
- **Conferences (booth-less; panels + curated dinners):** SyncSummit NYC Oct 1–2 2026 and LA Feb 8–9 2027, plus the AI+Sync Symposium July 14 2026 NYC ([SyncSummit events](https://syncsummit.com/events/)); Production Music Conference (PMA) ([SyncSummit/PMA](http://syncsummit.com/pma/)); Sync Con ([synccon.com](https://synccon.com/)); Screen Music Connect; A2IM Indie Week; MIDEM-successor events in Cannes. Budget: host a 12-seat "sync ops" dinner at each — this industry buys from people it has eaten with.
- **Associations / warm-intro rails:** AIMP chapters (LA/NY/Nashville/Atlanta — monthly panels; get on one) ([AIMP](https://aimp.org/)); NMPA annual meeting sponsorship later ([NMPA](https://www.nmpa.org/)); IMPF for European indies ([IMPF](https://www.impforum.org/links/)); PRO business-affairs contacts (ASCAP/BMI/PRS) as introducers, not buyers.
- **Trade press / content:** Music Business Worldwide (sponsored + earned), Music Week, Music Ally, Billboard Pro publishing briefs, and — enemy-adjacent but read by every buyer — the Synchtank blog's audience. Founder essays: "Why sync clearance still runs on Excel."
- **Outbound:** founder-led LinkedIn + email to Head of Sync titles; 3-touch sequence per account, personalized to a real placement ("your track X in trailer Y — how many emails did that clearance take?"). No SDRs yet; industry is relationship-dense and small — spray-and-pray burns the whole pond.
- **Community wedge:** the Catalog marketplace cohort (Beggars, Domino, Ninja Tune, Warp, Partisan ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/catalog-curated-music-licensing-sync-marketplace/))) is a pre-assembled cluster of sync-progressive indies who talk to each other — land one, referral-path to the rest.

### Outbound messaging angles (tested phrasing)
1. **Inbox-to-pipeline:** "Every sync request is a project with a deadline and a fee attached. Yours live in individual inboxes. When someone's on vacation, quotes expire."
2. **Expired money:** "What % of your quotes lapse without a decision? If you can't answer, that's the pitch."
3. **Split confirmation SLA:** "Post-acquisition catalogs: how long does split confirmation take per work? Loist makes it a tracked task with an owner, not a thread."
4. **M&A integration** (Primary Wave/Kobalt, Virgin/Downtown, Sony/Recognition): "Catalog migrations create 10,000 checkable tasks. We're the checklist."
5. **Audit/investor reporting** (funds: Round Hill, Recognition, Spirit/Lyric): "Show your LPs a real clearance pipeline, not a reconstructed spreadsheet."

## 5. Objections & answers

1. **"We live in email / our ERP already does this."**
   Answer: "Counterpoint/your royalty system is your system of record — we don't touch it. It records deals *after* they happen. Nothing you own manages the 3 weeks *before*: who's waiting on whom, which quote expires Friday, which co-publisher hasn't confirmed. Linear didn't replace GitHub; we don't replace Counterpoint." Proof: read-only import, email-in (forward a request to create a task) so they keep living in email while the pipeline builds itself.
2. **"Our splits and deal terms can't leave our systems."**
   Answer: tiered — (a) start pilots on non-sensitive workflow data only (statuses, deadlines, counterparties) with fee amounts optional/masked; (b) single-tenant deployment today is actually a *selling point*: "your own isolated instance, your own database"; (c) roadmap-commit SOC 2 Type II, EU/UK data residency, SSO/SAML, field-level encryption on splits; (d) DPA + no-training-on-your-data clause in every contract.
3. **"Music supervisors / our people won't use another tool."**
   Answer: supervisors never need accounts — they interact by email and shareable status links (the product's embed/streaming links help here). Internally: adoption pitch is "stop CCing five people"; pilot success criterion is defined as ≥70% of new requests entering via email-in with zero behavior change. Also: champion-led rollout on ONE team (film/TV sync) not company-wide.
4. **"You're too small / too risky."**
   Answer: (a) design-partner framing — "you get roadmap control precisely because we're small"; (b) data escrow + full export (CSV/JSON) contractually guaranteed — "you can leave any month with everything"; (c) month-to-month pilot, annual only after value proven; (d) name the reference cohort ASAP; (e) founder on-call support SLA that no enterprise vendor matches.
5. **(Bonus, will come up) "How is this different from Synchtank/DISCO?"**
   Answer: "They showcase and pitch your catalog outward. We manage the work inward — the clearance lifecycle, splits, registrations. Most of our design partners keep DISCO for pitching and use Loist for everything after a supervisor says 'yes, how much?'"

## 6. Pricing & packaging (sales lens)

- **Design partner (months 0–9):** $500–1,000/mo, 6-month term, 50% off Year-1 list locked in, in exchange for: weekly feedback call, 2 case-study quotes, logo rights on close of pilot. Paid because free pilots die in this industry; $6k is signable by an indie MD without a board conversation.
- **List packaging (land-and-expand by team, per-seat + platform fee):**
  - **Sync Team** — $99/user/mo (min 5 seats): clearance pipeline, email-in, status links, quote-expiry alerts.
  - **Publishing Ops** — $149/user/mo: + split confirmation workflows, registration tasks, works/parties data model, API.
  - **Enterprise** — from $50k/yr: SSO, audit log, data residency, dedicated instance, Counterpoint/Synchtank import, multi-office roll-up.
- **Land** on one sync team (5–10 seats ≈ $9–15k/yr) → **expand** to copyright/registration team → A&R → other offices/territories (peermusic-style multi-office orgs are the expansion goldmine).
- **Pilot structure:** 60–90 days, paid, single team, 3 mutually signed success metrics (e.g., 100% of new requests tracked; median request→quote time measured then cut 30%; zero quotes expired unactioned). Pre-agreed conversion price on day 1 so the pilot close isn't a second negotiation.
- **Do not** price on catalog size or % of sync fees in year 1 — it invites procurement math and rev-share allergy; revisit usage-based components only at enterprise tier.

## 7. Biggest GTM risks (ranked)

1. **Workflow-tool churn risk / "spreadsheet gravity":** the champion loves it, the team reverts to email in week 6. Mitigation: email-in ingestion is a launch-blocking product requirement, not a nice-to-have.
2. **M&A freeze:** 4 of the top-10 targets are mid-integration (Kobalt, Downtown, Recognition, HSG). Systems decisions freeze during integration — or get made once, org-wide, right after. Time approaches to the post-close 3–6-month window.
3. **Synchtank/Vistex adds a "tasks" module** before Loist has 10 logos — speed to reference cohort is the defense.
4. **Security bar arrives earlier than revenue:** even Tier A indies will ask for SOC 2 within 12 months; budget for it now.
